# CAD and PCB

Identify the editable PCB, schematic, generator and released fabrication package. Record the baseline revision, unit system, mounting datum and part suffix. Compare critical connector numbers and nets with the mating board. Cross-check a replacement IC against its manufacturer's land pattern, pad functions, voltage domains and sequencing. Keep bench and enclosure measurements distinct from CAD checks.

Run ERC, board DRC, schematic-to-board connectivity and mechanical interference checks on the same revision. Record errors, warnings, unrouted connections and unresolved reviewer decisions separately. Show whether the output is a design review or an orderable package.
