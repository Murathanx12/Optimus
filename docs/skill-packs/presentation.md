# Presentation and design

Start with the audience, decision, existing deck, format and brand sources. Fit content to the target slide dimensions, check measurements against source material and preserve editable elements. Render every slide at delivery size. Inspect text overflow, clipping, aspect ratio, contrast and bilingual glyphs where relevant. Reopen the delivered file.

## Aegis presentations

Use the [Optimus orbit and blackline specification](../../.claude/skills/orbit-blackline-visuals/SKILL.md) and the [Aegis visual identity](https://github.com/Murathanx12/Aegis-Finance/blob/main/docs/design/AEGIS_VISUAL_LANGUAGE_2026-10-07.md). The [original orbit](https://github.com/Murathanx12/Aegis-Finance/blob/main/docs/assets/aegis_loop.svg) and [historical results artwork](https://github.com/Murathanx12/Aegis-Finance/blob/main/docs/assets/paper_results_live.svg) are starting assets.

- Use a 16:9 black canvas (#000000), a restrained dot grid and thin corner brackets. Place real interface captures in lightly rounded frames. Use one dominant visual per slide and short captions.
- Use Inter for headlines and body text, a light, widely spaced wordmark, and a monospace face for dates, units and source labels. The white Aegis mark belongs on the cover with one audience question. Introduce people together on the team slide and give every co-founder equal space.
- Use electric blue (#4A8DFF) for the active model or forward path, light blue (#6FB0FF) for measured gains and orange (#FF8A1F) for learning or follow-up. Draw SPY as a dashed white line and controls in muted grey. State negative values with their sign.
- Show the research universe with its actual inputs, filters and recorded stage counts. Draw alternative hypotheses as branches. Identify which assumptions, dates and outcomes the reader can inspect. Show real product screenshots and diagram the read-only MCP connection when it is part of the business model.
- Use the recorded points for performance charts. Name the paper portfolio, benchmark, start and end dates, units, selection rule and material follow-up. Preserve gaps in the history. Keep pricing, commercial targets and recognised revenue distinct.
- Create the working design in Canva when requested. Keep headings, figures and captions editable. Preserve SVG masters outside the deck. Review every rendered Canva page at presentation size, especially time series, source crops and the team slide.
