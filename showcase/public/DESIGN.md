# Optimus showcase direction

Use the visual language of a navigable research notebook: indigo ink (#111629), slate panels (#1b2340), paper-white text (#edf1ff), periwinkle connections (#a8b6ff), cyan provenance (#8bd5db). Georgia supplies the expressive headline; system sans-serif keeps instructions readable. A single large, interactive evidence map makes the architecture memorable. Supporting content uses horizontal reading rows rather than repeated cards. Left-align copy, constrain paragraph measure, and let the diagram occupy the other half of the opening spread.

The initial dark-dashboard idea was revised to avoid a generic neon SaaS card grid. The diagram now explains actual storage and tool boundaries; its selectable stages reveal concrete, explicitly synthetic examples. No private brain export, backend endpoint, analytics or model call is shipped.
